- `generateShareHTML` has **no call site** in Davenport (dead code); the share page is rebuilt in Prompt 9.
- Davenport's generator uses `Math.random` in seven places and has no seed; Silvis uses a seeded PRNG (Prompt 4). Its best-of-N wrapper, single eligibility chokepoint, pre-assign-then-deal order, filter-then-fallback pools and 1-/2-hop chain-swap repair are the mechanisms borrowed conceptually.

## 2. Blast radius in `index-source.html` (weekly shift model / dropped features)
